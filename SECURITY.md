# Security Policy

## 🔒 Ethical Use & Legal Disclaimer

**APK Analyzer is intended for LEGAL SECURITY RESEARCH ONLY.**

This tool is designed for:
- ✅ Analyzing your **own** Android applications
- ✅ Security auditing of apps you **own or are authorized to test**
- ✅ Educational purposes and learning Android security
- ✅ Bug bounty programs with **explicit written authorization**

### ❌ What This Tool Must NOT Be Used For

- Cracking, pirating, or bypassing license verification of software you don't own
- Modifying apps for redistribution without the developer's consent
- Stealing API keys, credentials, or proprietary code from third-party apps
- Any activity that violates local, national, or international law

### ⚠️ User Responsibility

By using APK Analyzer, you agree that:
1. You are the **owner** of the APK or have **written authorization** to analyze it
2. You will **not** use the findings to exploit, harm, or profit from others' software
3. You are responsible for **complying with all applicable laws** in your jurisdiction
4. The authors and contributors of APK Analyzer are **not liable** for any misuse
